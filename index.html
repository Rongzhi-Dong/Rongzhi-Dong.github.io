About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of South Carolina. My research focuses on deep learning, machine learning, and AI for material science. I have experience developing generative models (Diffusion, Transformer, GAN, GNN, CNN, ResNet, LSTM) and applying them to materials generation, prediction, and discovery:contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.
